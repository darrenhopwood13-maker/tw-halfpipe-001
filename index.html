The headline: it has to be held down

  • The ramp presents a 40 m² face to the wind. It is a sail.
  • Self weight about 3.4 t, resisting roughly 10 kN on tarmac.
  • Wind force 19 to 30 kN depending on the shape factor assumed.
  • So: 3 to 7 tonnes of ballast, or ground anchors, before anyone rides it.
  • Uplift and the dynamic load of a rider are not checked here — the designer's.

Open inputs before anything is bought

  • The site — the wind map value comes from the location.
  • The shape factor — assumed 1.0 to 1.6, and it drives the ballast.
  • The height — adopted 2.00 m, because 5.00 = 1.00 flat + 2 x 2.00.
  • The ground — tarmac assumed; anchors may replace ballast.
  • Public use — decides which product standard applies.

Governing standards: BS EN 14974:2019 (skateparks), BS EN 13814-1:2019+A1:2024 (amusement devices), BS EN 1991-1-4, BS 5975-1:2024 / BS 5975-2:2024, CDM 2015.
